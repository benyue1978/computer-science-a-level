Conservative grouping only; preserve distinct meanings and technical words. Future JSON collections merge by normalized word and source ID without double-counting repeated imports.

Guest users keep their notes, learning state, draft and sent history in IndexedDB. Google sign-in enables account-level sync across browsers. The public coursebook collection and shared explanations live in Supabase; private explanations override shared ones for one account. A first sign-in copies the current guest state into that account. Accounts are row-level isolated. Export/import a versioned complete backup with validation and a review step before replacement. Surface storage failures and do not claim unsaved changes are saved.

Today defaults to five total words, adjustable through ten. Suggestions prioritize oldest due reviews, then frequent unseen words. Selection is explicit, editable and persistent. Notes accept Chinese/English and can be added or edited from any card in All words or Known list; the same word-level note appears in Today. Copy formats the chosen words, examples and notes. Feedback applies only to sent words; hiding is available for all. Hidden words remain searchable/restorable. All words provides search, source/status filters and pagination. A distinct Known list holds words marked already known/trivial; initially hidden common words remain separate and searchable. Known words can be returned to active words. History allows reopening copied lists. Imported source files contain source ID/name and word entries. Browser backups are manual, explained visibly.

## Shared and personal explanations

The explanation editor remains available in both the All words cards and the Today list. Every signed-in user can save a personal explanation; it is private to that account and takes precedence over a shared explanation for that user. A vocabulary administrator sees an additional “Publish for everyone” action in those same editors. Publishing replaces the shared explanation for that word, clears the administrator's personal override for that word, and shows the newly published shared explanation in the editor. Other users' personal overrides remain private and continue to take precedence. Non-administrators cannot publish or modify shared explanations; Supabase row-level security remains the enforcement boundary. Existing shared explanations are shown by default when there is no personal override.
