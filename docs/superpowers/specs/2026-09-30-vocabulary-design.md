# Daily vocabulary
Approved in conversation; implementation explicitly authorized on main.

Add /vocabulary to the existing Vite/React site and link from its home page. Ship an extracted JSON collection from the supplied coursebook. Keep ordinary vocabulary, common words (hidden initially), frequencies and short source examples; omit page/chapter references. Conservative grouping only; preserve distinct meanings and technical words. Future JSON collections merge by normalized word and source ID without double-counting repeated imports.

Use IndexedDB for the collection, notes, learning state, draft and sent history. No login or remote database. Export/import a versioned complete backup with validation and a review step before replacement. Surface storage failures and do not claim unsaved changes are saved.

Today defaults to five total words, adjustable through ten. Suggestions prioritize oldest due reviews, then frequent unseen words. Selection is explicit, editable and persistent. Notes accept Chinese/English. Copy formats the chosen words, examples and notes. Mark sent schedules first review after three days, records history and clears draft. Remembered advances intervals to 7, 14, 30 days (then stays at 30); needs practice resets to tomorrow. Feedback applies only to sent words; hiding is available for all. Hidden words remain searchable/restorable. All words provides search, source/status filters and pagination. A distinct Known list holds words marked already known/trivial; initially hidden common words remain separate and searchable. Known words can be returned to active words. History allows reopening copied lists. Imported source files contain source ID/name and word entries. Browser backups are manual, explained visibly.
